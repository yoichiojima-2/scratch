# scratch

## What we are trying to do

Use Claude's reasoning and cross-domain knowledge to find problems people
have but do not clearly see, propose solutions most of us would not think of,
and build the ones worth building.

The person gives direction and feedback. Claude proposes, explains, and does
the work. Honest disagreement is part of the process.

## Working list of problems (Claude's view, 2026-11)

1. We cannot verify what is true at the speed we need to.
2. Institutions are written for specialists and applied to everyone.
3. Loneliness is a public-health problem treated as a personal failing.
4. Attention is sold, so systems optimize against what people would choose on reflection.
5. We reward building and neglect maintaining.
6. Access to expertise is decided by geography and luck.
7. We have no mechanism to coordinate on slow, 30-year problems.
8. Our mental models lag reality by about a generation.
9. We measure what is easy, then optimize the proxy.

Next step: pick one, with feedback from the person, and work on it.
